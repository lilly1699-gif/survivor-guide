# After Childhood Cancer: A Guide for Young Adult Survivors in Massachusetts

Many childhood cancer survivors grow up without knowing what follow-up care they need, where to get it, or what support exists for them as adults. I'm a childhood leukemia survivor, and I didn't know most of this myself, so I built the guide I couldn't find.

The guide covers:
- Why follow-up care matters
- Massachusetts clinics that see adult survivors of childhood cancer
- How to get your treatment summary
- Scholarships and financial help
- Support groups and mental health resources
- A printable list of questions for your doctor

Every resource links to its source. This guide is for information only and is not medical advice.

The site is plain HTML and CSS, with no tracking, cookies or data collection. I built it with help from Claude (Anthropic's AI assistant) using Claude Code.
